# Emerald Tide

Create a world-class, award-winning interactive agricultural biotech web experience for "TERA-WET" (terawet.com), inspired by the cinematic design of Resn's cornrevolution.resn.global/#science.

### 🎨 DESIGN SYSTEM & AESTHETICS (Dark Bio-Tech Luxury):
- Background: Deep obsidian emerald gradients (from #00120a via #002415 to #003822) with subtle floating water particle effects (interactive canvas / Three.js particle dust).
- Typography: High-impact condensed ultra-bold headline font (like Anton, Syne, or Bebas Neue) with sleek sans-serif body text (Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans).
- Color Palette: 
  * Primary: Luminous Emerald (#10b981 / #059669)
  * Accent / Water: Cyan Hydro Glow (#06b6d4 / #38bdf8)
  * Background: Deep Forest Obsidian (#00140c)
  * Surface: Glassmorphic dark card panels with border border-emerald-500/20 and backdrop-blur-md.

### 🌟 KEY SECTIONS & ARCHITECTURE:

1. HERO SECTION ("TERAWET. REVOLUTIONIZED."):
   - Fullscreen viewport with massive typography: "TERAWET. REVOLUTIONIZED."
   - Subtitle: "Superabsorbent polymer hydrogel technology that transforms drought-prone soil into fertile water reservoirs for 7-10 years."
   - Central Interactive Visual: An interactive 3D / Canvas hydrogel crystal element that reacts to cursor movement, with glowing water droplets and root capillaries weaving around it.
   - Quick CTA buttons: [Explore Catalog] and [The Science of Hydrogel].
   - Sleek minimalist header with Terawet logo, language switcher (UA / EN / RU), and menu toggle.

2. THE SCIENCE SECTION (#science - Interactive Microscopic Experience):
   - Headline: "THE SCIENCE OF CELLULAR HYDRATION"
   - Subtitle: "How cross-linked polyacrylamide superabsorbent matrices retain 400x their weight in water."
   - Interactive 4-Stage Storytelling Slider / Hotspots:
     * Stage 1: Absorption Matrix (Crystals expand 400x in 15 minutes).
     * Stage 2: Moisture Reservoir (Prevents evaporation and gravitational water loss).
     * Stage 3: Root Osmotic Exchange (Plants absorb moisture on demand, reducing irrigation by 50%).
     * Stage 4: 10-Year Lifecycle (Eco-friendly, biodegradable over 7-10 years).
   - Include comparison metrics: Water Savings (50%), Yield Increase (+25-40%), Survival Rate (+98%).

3. PRODUCT CATALOG (Tera-Wet Product Matrix):
   - Tera-Wet T-400 (Agricultural grade for soil & fields)
   - Tera-Wet T-100 (Fine powder for seed coating & root dipping)
   - Retail & Industrial packaging: 1 kg, 5 kg, 25 kg bags.
   - Each card features clean glassmorphism, technical specs, application rates, and an "Order / Request Quote" modal.

4. APPLICATION CALCULATOR:
   - Interactive dosage calculator: Select crop/field type (Trees, Vineyards, Wheat/Corn, Vegetables, Lawn) + area (hectares or sq. meters) -> Automatically calculates recommended kg of Tera-Wet and estimated water savings.

5. VOICE AI CONSULTANT WIDGET:
   - Embed an elegant floating ElevenLabs Voice AI agent trigger in the bottom corner (as used in the current Terawet site) with pulse animation for 24/7 instant voice consultation.

6. CONTACT & ORDER FORM:
   - Premium dark contact section with form validation, WhatsApp/Telegram/Email contacts, and company info.
